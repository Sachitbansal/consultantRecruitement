About GOP

A vertical of SBSSdigital, built by senior operators.

By GOP Editorial · Published 23 October 2026 · Updated 23 October 2026

Who is GOP? GOP (Growth Operating Partners) is the growth consulting and strategy execution vertical of SBSSdigital. Several cohort mates from the IIM Calcutta Executive Programme have come together to build and scale GOP, an operator-led collective of senior professionals with 100+ years of combined hands-on experience.

Where GOP comes from

GOP is the growth consulting and strategy execution vertical of SBSSdigital, an enterprise solutions venture. GOP was founded in 2021, when several cohort mates from the IIM Calcutta Executive Programme came together to build and scale it, drawing on senior careers across manufacturing, textiles and apparel, telecom, media, lighting, logistics, fintech, retail and finance.

The idea is simple. Promoter-led manufacturers need senior, hands-on help, but few can afford six or more full-time senior specialists. GOP provides that depth fractionally and on demand, working inside the business against agreed outcomes.

Who we work with

GOP works with promoter-led and family-owned manufacturers, typically with revenue of INR 150-500 crore. Sectors include fans and motors, electricals, heat exchangers, engineering components, home appliances, paper and packaging, FMCG and consumer durables. What these businesses share is more important than the sector: strong products and customer relationships, a promoter who still makes most of the decisions, and systems that were built for a smaller company.

That combination produces a familiar set of symptoms. Costing is approximate. MIS arrives late and is argued over. Working capital tightens every quarter. Growth depends on the promoter's own relationships. And when an investor or a large customer asks for numbers that hold up under scrutiny, the business is not ready to hand them over. See who we help for the full picture, including when GOP is not the right fit.

What we believe

  • Operators, not advisors. We do not deliver a strategy deck and walk away. We work with the business to make the transformation happen.
  • Outcomes first. Targets are agreed before work starts, measured on a shared scoreboard and reviewed every week. We are judged on what changes in the business, not on what we write about it.
  • Build on what works. We start with the people and practices that already deliver, and add structure around them.
  • Systems that last. We leave a scoreboard, routines and trained owners so the result does not depend on us or on the promoter's attention.

Investment se pehle bhi, investment ke baad bhi

Many of our clients are thinking about private equity, a strategic partner or an SME listing. GOP works on both sides of that moment. Before investment, the work is about becoming credible: reporting that reconciles, governance that does not depend on one person, and a growth plan backed by evidence. After investment, the work is about delivery: hitting the KPIs the investor underwrote, and building the management depth that lets the business keep growing. What GOP does not do

We are clear about our lane. GOP does not provide statutory audit, legal, tax or regulatory opinions, and we are not merchant bankers or placement agents. Where an engagement touches those areas, such as preparing for a listing or a transaction, we work alongside the qualified advisers the company appoints and make sure the business is ready for them.

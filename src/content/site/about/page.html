Growth Requires More Than Advice. It Requires Execution.

Growth Operating Partners exists to help promoter-led businesses build stronger operating foundations, grow profitably and prepare for their next stage.

By GOP Editorial · Published 23 October 2026 · Updated 24 October 2026

Who is GOP? GOP (Growth Operating Partners) is the growth consulting and strategy execution vertical of SBSSdigital. Several cohort mates from the IIM Calcutta Executive Programme have come together to build and scale GOP, an operator-led collective of senior professionals with 100+ years of combined hands-on experience.

GOP brings together relevant professional experience, institutional learning and an operator-led perspective to support business growth, institutional readiness and value creation.

Our purpose

To help businesses convert ambition into disciplined execution and stronger business performance.

Our philosophy: Investment se pehle bhi. Investment ke baad bhi.

GOP believes that the work of building business value begins before capital is raised and continues after investment is made.

Before investment, the focus may be on growth, profitability, management systems and investment readiness. When capital is needed, GOP helps the business prepare its pitch and take it to suitable mid-market PE and VC investors. After investment, the focus shifts to executing the investment thesis, improving performance and measuring progress against agreed priorities.

Our principles

  • Business outcomes first. Start with the business challenge and the outcome sought.
  • Execution matters. Convert plans into initiatives, ownership and action.
  • Evidence over assertion. Use verifiable information, clear measures and transparent attribution.
  • Partnership with accountability. Work collaboratively while maintaining clarity on responsibilities.
  • Long-term value. Focus on improvements that can strengthen the business beyond an individual intervention.

Where GOP comes from

GOP is the growth consulting and strategy execution vertical of SBSSdigital, an enterprise solutions venture. It was founded in 2021, when several cohort mates from the IIM Calcutta Executive Programme came together to build and scale it, drawing on senior careers across manufacturing, textiles and apparel, telecom, media, lighting, logistics, fintech, retail and finance. GOP is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by IIM Calcutta, and team members' former employers do not endorse GOP.

The idea is simple. Promoter-led businesses need senior, hands-on help, but few can afford six or more full-time senior specialists. GOP provides that depth fractionally and on demand, working inside the business against agreed outcomes.

Who we work with

Business promoters and their leadership teams, primarily of promoter-led businesses with approximately INR 150-500 crore in revenue; prospective investors; and PE/VC funds with portfolio companies to build. Manufacturing is where the team's experience runs deepest: fans and motors, electricals, heat exchangers, engineering components, home appliances, paper and packaging, FMCG and consumer durables. See for business promoters, for investors and for PE/VC funds.

Our credibility

GOP's credibility should be shown through evidence, not assertion:

  • Institutional qualifications and executive education
  • Relevant professional experience
  • Business challenges previously addressed by team members
  • Documented examples of work and attributable achievements
  • GOP's own client outcomes as they are established

We keep prior-career achievements separate from GOP client results. See experience and outcomes and meet the people behind GOP.

What GOP does not do

We are clear about our lane. GOP does not invest its own capital. It does not provide statutory audit, legal, tax or regulatory opinions, and it is not a merchant banker or a certifying authority. When GOP helps a business raise growth capital, the transaction documents, securities matters and any regulated activity are handled by the qualified advisers and registered intermediaries the company appoints. GOP works alongside them on the business and operating side.

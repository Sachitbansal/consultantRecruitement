What results has GOP delivered? GOP's practice leads have delivered the following anonymised results in earlier engagements across manufacturing: capacity doubled in heat exchangers, changeover cut by 45% in a press shop, throughput up 50% in fabrication, and manpower optimised by 32% in a new fan plant.

These results were delivered by the senior practitioners who now lead GOP's manufacturing and supply chain practice. Client names are withheld by agreement, and we describe each client by type.

Materials handling redesign: 60% manpower optimisation

A plant was brought live with 60% manpower optimisation through conveyorised flow.

How these results were achieved

None of these gains came from a large capital programme. They came from the same small set of methods, applied with discipline on the shop floor:

  • Flow before machines. Mapping how material actually moves, setting takt time to customer demand and redesigning layout and sequence for pull, before any new equipment is considered.
  • Changeover reduction. Separating the work that needs the machine stopped from the work that does not, then standardising the routine.
  • Design for flow from day one. In new plants, laying out lines, handling and conveyors around the product flow, which is where most of the manpower and space difference comes from.
  • Daily management. A short daily review at the line against a few KPIs, so the gain is still there a year later.

How we measure results

A result only counts if it was measured the same way before and after, on the same definitions, and held over a sustained period rather than a single good week. Baselines are set during the diagnostic, agreed with the client's own team and tracked on a shared scoreboard. This is also why results are shown as before-and-after figures rather than as general claims of improvement.

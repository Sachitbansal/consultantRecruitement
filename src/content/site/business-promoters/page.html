How does GOP help business promoters? GOP works with promoters of established businesses, typically INR 150-500 crore in revenue, to find the growth and margin priorities that matter, build the management systems that reduce dependence on the promoter, and prepare for growth capital. Senior operators work alongside your team, against measures agreed at the start.

When Growth Needs a New Operating Model.

Your business may have reached a stage where entrepreneurial energy alone is no longer enough. Common challenges include:

  • Growth is slower than the market opportunity.
  • Revenue growth is not translating into sufficient profitability.
  • Sales performance varies significantly across teams or markets.
  • Customer concentration creates business risk.
  • The organisation needs to expand into new products, channels or geographies.
  • Operational inefficiencies constrain margins.
  • The promoter remains central to too many daily decisions.
  • Management reporting does not provide a sufficiently clear view of performance.
  • The business wants to attract capital but is not yet adequately prepared.

GOP starts by understanding which of these challenges matter most to your business. In manufacturing, where most of our experience lies, they often show up together: costing is approximate, MIS arrives late, working capital tightens every quarter and new business depends on the promoter's own relationships.

What Working with GOP Looks Like

  • Understand the business. Establish the current position, ambitions and constraints.
  • Agree on priorities. Identify the initiatives that matter most.
  • Build an execution plan. Assign owners, timelines and measurable outcomes.
  • Work with leadership. Support implementation, resolve bottlenecks and review progress.
  • Build organisational capability. Strengthen routines and systems so improvements can continue.

We do not promise a specific revenue or profitability increase before we understand the business. Targets are set once the diagnostic has established the baseline. See our approach.

Why This Matters

A stronger business can be better positioned to:

  • Grow with greater discipline.
  • Improve the quality of earnings.
  • Use capital more effectively.
  • Reduce operational dependence on the promoter.
  • Build management depth.
  • Prepare for strategic partnerships or investment.
  • Create long-term enterprise value.

These are intended benefits, not guaranteed outcomes.

When GOP may not be the right fit

  • You want a strategy report only, with no implementation support.
  • The promoter is not willing to delegate decisions or back changes.
  • The business is at a very early stage without established operations.

Frequently asked questions

What size of business is GOP best suited for?

GOP's primary target segment is promoter-led businesses with approximately INR 150-500 crore in revenue. That is a focus, not an eligibility rule: suitability depends on the business challenge, leadership commitment and scope of the engagement.

Does GOP only advise, or does it help with execution?

GOP is operator-led. The engagement is designed around practical priorities, execution support and performance reviews, with the exact scope agreed at the outset.

Can GOP help if my business is growing but margins are under pressure?

Yes. The initial discussion can explore the drivers of margin pressure, commercial performance, operating costs, productivity and the opportunities for improvement.

Can GOP help prepare my business for investment?

GOP can help strengthen growth plans, management systems, governance, reporting and investment preparedness. Readiness does not guarantee that an investment will occur.

Does GOP provide growth capital directly?

No. GOP does not invest its own money. It helps you prepare the pitch deck, take the pitch to mid-market PE and VC investors and work through the raise, within an agreed mandate. It cannot guarantee that capital will be raised.

How does an engagement begin?

It begins with a discussion of the business, its objectives and the challenges that need attention, usually as a free diagnostic conversation. If there is a fit, both sides agree on the scope, responsibilities and expected outputs.

How GOP works

Diagnostic, roadmap, implementation. Operators embedded against KPIs.

By GOP Editorial · Published 23 October 2026 · Updated 23 October 2026

How does GOP work? Every engagement runs in three stages: a fixed-scope diagnostic of one to two weeks, a sequenced roadmap with an owner for every action, and implementation by senior operators embedded in your team. Targets are agreed before work starts and reviewed every week on a shared KPI scoreboard.

1. Diagnostic (one to two weeks)

A pre-audit with a fixed scope and timeline. It looks at true margin, customers, pricing, sales process, demand sources, operations, supply chain, working capital, organisation and governance. You receive a scorecard of strengths and gaps, a quantified gap to your target and a first view of priorities. Read what a growth diagnostic should cover.

2. Roadmap

A sequenced, costed plan with an owner for every action and dates against your target. The roadmap follows a deliberate order: cost and value first, then demand, then reach, then the system that holds it in place.

3. Implementation

GOP embeds full-time resources into your team, working against predefined KPIs and KRAs, not a periodic advisory check-in. A weekly review keeps every metric visible, and a named owner stands behind each one. Operators work with your managers, develop them, and hand over a working system.

The scoreboard

Targets are agreed before work starts and reviewed weekly. We do not commit to numbers we have not earned the right to see, so baselines are filled in during the diagnostic. From then on, every review starts with the scoreboard: what moved, what did not, and who owns the next step.

What we expect from you

  • A promoter or business head who owns the targets and backs the changes
  • A named internal counterpart
  • Access to data, including reports rather than shared passwords where possible
  • Patience through the first quarter

Operators who deliver

GOP's operators bring plant, sales, marketing, supply chain and governance experience. They have run businesses, so they understand the people on the ground and know how to enlist them as enablers of change. See the team and how GOP compares with consultants.

Frequently asked questions

How long does a diagnostic take?

One to two weeks, with the scope agreed in writing before it starts.

Do you commit to targets before you start?

Targets are agreed once the diagnostic has set the baselines, then tracked on a weekly scoreboard with a named owner for each metric.

Who works on my business?

Experienced operators who are named in the proposal, supported by a delivery team. They work inside your business against agreed KPIs.

What happens when the engagement ends?

You keep the scoreboard, routines and trained owners, so results do not depend on GOP or on promoter attention.

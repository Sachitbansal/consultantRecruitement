How does GOP help PE and VC funds? GOP supports portfolio companies after investment by turning the investment thesis into a prioritised plan of initiatives, owners and measures, and by working with leadership to deliver it. GOP also prepares promoter-led businesses to raise growth capital and brings mandated, investment-ready businesses to mid-market PE and VC investors.

The Portfolio Challenge

Investment theses often depend on several things happening after capital is deployed:

  • Revenue growth accelerates.
  • Commercial productivity improves.
  • Margins strengthen.
  • Operations scale efficiently.
  • Management capability develops.
  • Reporting and governance mature.
  • Capital is deployed against agreed priorities.
  • Performance issues are identified early enough to respond.

The challenge is translating these expectations into coordinated execution, especially in promoter-led businesses where many decisions still run through one person.

Engagement Model

A potential engagement may include:

  1. Initial alignment with the fund and portfolio leadership.
  2. Review of the investment thesis and business context.
  3. Assessment of the most material operating opportunities.
  4. Development of a prioritised value-creation plan.
  5. Agreement on measures, responsibilities and governance.
  6. Execution support and progress reviews.
  7. Periodic reassessment of priorities and impact.

The final scope depends on the portfolio company, the fund's mandate and the capabilities required.

What Good Looks Like

The aim is to improve clarity and execution around:

  • Growth priorities
  • Commercial performance
  • Margin improvement
  • Operating discipline
  • Management accountability
  • Reporting quality
  • Investment-readiness milestones
  • Realisation of agreed value-creation initiatives

GOP does not promise investment returns, exits or specific improvements. It commits to a clear plan, agreed measures and transparent reporting of progress.

Frequently asked questions

Can GOP work directly with a portfolio company's leadership team?

That can be considered where the fund and company agree on the mandate, roles and responsibilities.

Can GOP support a pre-investment assessment?

Potentially, subject to scope, information access, independence requirements and applicable professional obligations.

Can GOP work alongside existing consultants or functional experts?

Yes. The engagement can be designed to complement existing capabilities, avoid duplication and clarify accountability.

How are results measured?

The parties agree on a baseline, measures, reporting frequency and attribution approach before implementation, and review progress on a shared scoreboard.

New Business Development

Lever 3 of GOP's Five Levers. How can a promoter-led manufacturer build a predictable new-customer pipeline?

By GOP Editorial · Published 23 October 2026 · Updated 23 October 2026

How can a promoter-led manufacturer build a predictable new-customer pipeline? GOP builds a structured engine for finding, qualifying and converting new customers, including OEM, supplier-ecosystem and export accounts, so that new business no longer depends on the promoter's personal network. That means named target accounts, mapped buyers, multi-channel outreach, results-led pitches and a funnel reviewed weekly with an owner for every lead.

The problem this lever solves

Most promoter-led manufacturers have grown through relationships and referrals. That is a strength, but it caps growth at the promoter's bandwidth. There is rarely a named list of target accounts, a defined funnel, or an owner for each lead.

Proposals often read as capability brochures, and follow-ups depend on memory. A predictable pipeline needs target selection, buyer mapping, regular outreach, a pitch that leads with results, and a funnel reviewed every week.

Five interventions

a. Target-account and segment selection

We select segments and named accounts where your product has a clear edge, and size the opportunity so effort goes where conversion is most likely.

b. Outbound programme across channels

We run structured outreach through warm referrals, calling and email campaigns, events and partner-sourced data lists, with every referral source tracked.

c. Proposal and pitch systems that lead with results

We build short, outcome-led pitch materials and proposal templates that show what changes for the buyer, with proof, rather than listing capabilities.

d. CRM and funnel stages with clear ownership

We define funnel stages from prospect listed through to terms agreed, assign a primary and secondary owner to every lead, and review the funnel weekly.

e. OEM-anchor and supplier-ecosystem entry strategies

Where an OEM or large buyer sponsors access to its supplier base, we help structure the approach so that suppliers see a clear business case for engaging.

AI deployment from top to bottom of the lead funnel: account research, prospect scoring, outreach drafting and follow-up reminders that keep every lead moving.

What changes in the first 90 days

  • Days 1-30: Define target segments and a first list of named accounts. Set up funnel stages and owners.
  • Days 31-60: Launch outreach in two or three channels. Build the pitch pack and proposal template.
  • Days 61-90: Review conversion at each stage, refine targeting and messaging, and set quarterly pipeline targets.

How progress is measured

  • New qualified leads per month by source
  • Conversion between funnel stages
  • Average sales cycle
  • Win rate
  • Pipeline value by stage
  • Share of revenue from new accounts

Who this lever is for

This lever suits manufacturers entering new segments, adding OEM or export accounts, or trying to grow beyond the promoter's own network.

How GOP works on this lever

GOP works as operators alongside the promoter, not as external advisors. Every engagement starts with a fixed-scope diagnostic, moves to a sequenced roadmap with owners, and then to implementation inside your team against agreed KPIs. See how we work, or read about the other four levers.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Relying on the promoter's calls as the entire pipeline.
  • Treating all enquiries equally with no qualification.
  • Sending one brochure to every prospect.
  • Reviewing the funnel only when sales are short.

Questions to ask yourself before starting

  • Do we have a named list of target accounts?
  • Does every lead have an owner and a stage?
  • Does our pitch lead with an outcome and proof?

What good looks like after a year

The company knows how many qualified leads it needs each month to hit its revenue target, and where they will come from. The promoter still opens doors at the senior level, but follow-up no longer depends on their calendar. Proposals are built from a standard structure and lead with results. The weekly funnel review takes thirty minutes and ends with actions. New customers, including OEM and export accounts, arrive through a process the team can run and improve on its own.

A hypothetical scenario

Hypothetical, for illustration only. Not a client case. A component maker wants to supply a large OEM but has no named contacts beyond one purchase officer. The team selects a short list of target accounts where the company has a clear edge, maps decision-makers and influencers in each, and runs structured outreach through referrals, calling, email and an industry event. Pitches lead with a specific outcome and proof rather than a capability brochure. Every lead is tracked through defined stages with an owner. Within a quarter the funnel shows where conversions stall, so the team fixes that stage instead of guessing.

Frequently asked questions

How many leads do we need?

That depends on average order value, conversion and cycle length. We work backwards from your revenue target to the number of qualified leads required each month.

Can referrals alone sustain growth?

Referrals convert well but are hard to scale. They work best alongside a structured outbound routine and a clear funnel.

Do you replace our sales team?

No. We set up the system and coach your team to run it, and we can add execution capacity during the build.

What does GOP do? GOP works with promoter-led businesses on five value-creation levers: profitable growth, commercial excellence, operational excellence, governance and investment readiness, and growth capital with post-investment value creation. Digital growth and AI enablement supports all five. Senior operators deliver the work inside the business against agreed measures.

Five levers and one enabler

Each lever addresses a different driver of business value. Most engagements combine two or three, chosen from the diagnostic and sequenced so that cost and value are repaired before money is spent on demand or expansion.

How the levers fit together

The order matters. Margin and operating improvements come first, because they improve every rupee of revenue that follows. Commercial and market expansion come next, on repaired economics. Governance and investment readiness turn a stronger business into one that investors can trust, and growth capital, raised on that foundation, funds the next stage. After investment, the same levers become the value-creation plan.

Three capabilities behind every lever

  • Strategy: where to play and where to create disproportionate value.
  • Execution: what changes across sales, marketing, operations, supply chain, organisation and governance, and who makes it happen.
  • Value creation: how those changes translate into revenue growth, margin expansion, working-capital improvement, governance and enterprise value.

Who the levers are for

Business promoters use them to grow and prepare for capital. PE/VC funds use them to deliver an investment thesis in a portfolio company. Investors use GOP's operating lens to understand what a business needs next. See for business promoters, for PE/VC funds and for investors.

Frequently asked questions

How many levers does a typical engagement use?

Most engagements start with one or two levers chosen from the diagnostic, then add others as results build.

Do we have to take all of them?

No. You can start with the lever that addresses your largest gap, and add others as needed.

Is digital and AI work a separate project?

Usually not. It supports the other levers and is measured by the same business measures.

Make Every Commercial Effort Work Harder.

Improve the effectiveness of sales, marketing, customer acquisition and market development by connecting commercial priorities with execution and measurable performance.

By GOP Editorial · Published 23 October 2026 · Updated 24 October 2026

How does a business earn more from its commercial effort? GOP first recovers margin already inside the order book by fixing pricing, discounts, key-account plans and the sales process. It then builds the brand and demand engine: clear positioning, answer-first content, search and AI-search visibility and channel strategy, with customer acquisition cost tracked against margin.

The problem this lever solves

In many promoter-led businesses, the promoter has personally negotiated the big accounts for years. Prices, discounts and terms live in relationships and memory, and nobody has a clean view of which customers and products make money after freight, credit, returns and service. At the same time, buyers now research suppliers online, increasingly through AI assistants, before speaking to anyone. A business with an unclear position and no visible expertise is invisible at the moment of choice.

The order matters. Spending on new demand before pricing and margin are fixed multiplies the leak, because new orders arrive on the same weak economics.

Potential focus areas

Pricing, product mix and discount discipline

We build a true-economics view by customer, product and channel, compare list price with realised price, and set discount bands with an escalation path, so concessions become decisions instead of habits.

Sales effectiveness and pipeline management

We structure account plans for the customers that matter, redesign the process from enquiry to order, and review pipeline quality and coverage on a weekly scoreboard with an owner for every metric.

Customer, channel and retention strategy

We set targets, margins and service standards for distributors and channel partners, and track retention and share of wallet in key accounts.

Brand positioning

We define who you serve, what you do better than alternatives and the proof that backs it, then write it in plain language that buyers and search systems can quote.

Digital demand generation and marketing effectiveness

We build answer-first pages with structured data, plan content around real buyer questions, assess marketplace channels, and measure cost per qualified enquiry and conversion by source against a margin floor.

How we work

Understand the current commercial model, identify performance gaps, prioritise improvements and build an execution rhythm around agreed measures.

  • Days 1-30: Build the customer and product profitability view. Audit positioning, website and listings.
  • Days 31-60: Introduce discount rules and approval limits. Start account plans. Publish core pages.
  • Days 61-90: Run the weekly scoreboard. Start measured demand pilots on products that clear the margin floor.

How progress is measured

  • Price realisation against list
  • Gross margin by customer and product
  • Discount as a share of sales
  • Order intake and win rate
  • Cost per qualified enquiry and conversion by source
  • Presence in AI assistant answers for target buyer questions

GOP works as operators alongside the promoter, not as external advisors. Every engagement starts with a fixed-scope diagnostic, moves to a sequenced roadmap with owners, and then to implementation inside your team against agreed measures. See our approach, or read about the other capabilities.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Giving similar discounts to every large customer regardless of cost to serve.
  • Letting exceptions be approved by the person who benefits from the order.
  • Buying paid traffic before the website, listings and offer are ready.
  • Measuring traffic instead of qualified enquiries.

What good looks like after a year

The promoter no longer negotiates every large order personally, because the sales team works inside agreed bands and knows when to escalate. Finance and sales read the same profitability view. Buyers who have never met the promoter find the company, understand what it is best at and arrive at the first meeting half-convinced, and marketing spend is a decision made against margin.

A hypothetical scenario

Hypothetical, for illustration only. Not a client case. A manufacturer sees margin slip while volumes rise, and has a thin website that rarely appears when buyers search. A profitability review shows a handful of large accounts taking deep discounts and long credit. New discount bands and a weekly scoreboard recover margin from the existing order book first. Only then does the team publish answer-first pages and start paid pilots, on the products that clear a margin floor.

Frequently asked questions

Can GOP help improve sales productivity?

The engagement can examine sales processes, customer prioritisation, pipeline quality, sales coverage and performance management.

Can GOP support marketing strategy?

Yes, within the agreed scope. Work may include market positioning, demand generation, channel priorities and the measurement of marketing effectiveness.

Does GOP replace the existing sales or marketing team?

The engagement should clarify roles and complement the client's organisation. Any operating responsibilities must be explicitly agreed.

Will tighter discount rules lose us customers?

Rules are set with data on which discounted deals were genuinely at risk. Most firms find that fewer deals needed the concession than the sales team assumed.

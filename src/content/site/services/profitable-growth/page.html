Find the Next Engine of Profitable Growth.

Growth should create business value, not just increase the top line. GOP helps businesses assess their growth opportunities, prioritise the right markets and translate ambition into an actionable plan.

By GOP Editorial · Published 23 October 2026 · Updated 24 October 2026

How can a promoter-led business find its next source of profitable growth? GOP assesses where growth can come from, including existing customers, new segments, products, channels and geographies, and ranks each option by commercial attractiveness and execution feasibility. It then builds a structured engine to win new customers, including OEM and export accounts, so new business no longer depends on the promoter's network.

What we help address

  • Slow or inconsistent growth
  • Limited market penetration
  • Customer concentration
  • Dependence on a narrow product portfolio
  • Expansion into new geographies or channels
  • Weak prioritisation of growth opportunities
  • Growth without adequate profitability

Most promoter-led businesses have grown through relationships and referrals. That is a strength, but it caps growth at the promoter's bandwidth. There is rarely a named list of target accounts, a defined funnel or an owner for each lead, and expansion decisions are made on instinct rather than on evidence about margin and capability.

Areas of work

Market and opportunity assessment

We size the realistic opportunity in each market or segment you could serve, using available evidence on demand, competition and your own capability, so effort goes where conversion is most likely.

Customer and segment prioritisation

We select segments and named accounts where your product has a clear edge, and check that the margin after cost to serve justifies the effort.

Product, category, channel and geography strategy

We evaluate adjacent products and categories, new channels and new geographies against what the business can deliver well, and sequence them so one expansion does not starve another.

A structured new-customer engine

We run outreach through warm referrals, calling, email, events and partner-sourced lists, build short outcome-led pitch materials, and define funnel stages from prospect listed to terms agreed, with a primary and secondary owner for every lead. Where an OEM or large buyer sponsors access to its supplier base, we help structure the approach.

Revenue and profitability planning, with execution reviews

We turn the chosen growth initiatives into a revenue and margin plan with owners and milestones, and review it with leadership on a fixed rhythm.

Digital and AI support: account research, prospect scoring, outreach drafting and follow-up reminders that keep every lead moving. See digital growth and AI.

Our approach

  1. Understand the current growth model.
  2. Identify the most relevant growth opportunities.
  3. Evaluate commercial attractiveness and execution feasibility.
  4. Prioritise initiatives.
  5. Assign owners and milestones.
  6. Track progress and adapt.

How progress is measured

  • New qualified leads per month by source
  • Conversion between funnel stages
  • Win rate and average sales cycle
  • Pipeline value by stage
  • Share of revenue from new accounts, segments or geographies
  • Gross margin on new business

How GOP works on this lever

GOP works as operators alongside the promoter, not as external advisors. Every engagement starts with a fixed-scope diagnostic, moves to a sequenced roadmap with owners, and then to implementation inside your team against agreed measures. See our approach, or read about the other capabilities.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Relying on the promoter's calls as the entire pipeline.
  • Entering a new market because a competitor did.
  • Treating all enquiries equally with no qualification.
  • Chasing volume that dilutes margin.
  • Reviewing the funnel only when sales are short.

What good looks like after a year

The company knows how many qualified leads it needs each month to hit its revenue target, and where they will come from. The promoter still opens doors at the senior level, but follow-up no longer depends on their calendar. Expansion choices are documented with the evidence behind them, and new customers, including OEM and export accounts, arrive through a process the team can run and improve on its own.

A hypothetical scenario

Hypothetical, for illustration only. Not a client case. A component maker wants to supply a large OEM but has no named contacts beyond one purchase officer, and is also considering a second product category. The team ranks the options on margin and feasibility and decides to defer the new category. It selects a short list of target accounts, maps decision-makers in each and runs structured outreach. Pitches lead with a specific outcome and proof. Within a quarter the funnel shows where conversions stall, so the team fixes that stage instead of guessing.

Frequently asked questions

Can GOP help identify new markets?

GOP can help evaluate potential markets and prioritise opportunities based on available evidence, business capabilities and commercial considerations.

Does market expansion always mean entering a new geography?

No. Growth can come from existing customers, new customer segments, additional products, channels, pricing or better execution.

Will GOP guarantee a revenue target?

No. Targets should be agreed only after reviewing the business context. Outcomes depend on execution and external factors.

Do you replace our sales team?

No. We set up the system and coach your team to run it, and can add execution capacity during the build.

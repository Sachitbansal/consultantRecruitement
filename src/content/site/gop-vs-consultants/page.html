GOP vs consulting firms: operators, not advisors

Consultants advise. Operators deliver.

By GOP Editorial · Published 23 October 2026 · Updated 23 October 2026

How is GOP different from a consulting firm? Consulting firms bring consultants who are strong in theory and presentation. GOP brings operators who have delivered real results, understand the ground reality, and know how to work with the people inside a business to make change happen. They work inside your team against KPIs agreed upfront, and leave behind a working system rather than a report.

Why the team is the difference

GOP's advantage is not a framework or a process that anyone can copy. It is the combination of people. Replicating it would mean hiring six or more senior specialists full-time just to approximate what GOP offers fractionally and on demand. Three things set the team apart:

  • Pedigree: alumni of leading engineering and management institutions, including a shared IIM Calcutta Executive Programme cohort.
  • Brands run, not only advised: senior roles in manufacturing, textiles, retail, media, telecom, lighting, logistics and fintech businesses.
  • Domain spread: from shop-floor operations to commercial engines and board-level governance.

Where other firms are the right choice

If you need a one-off market study, a specific second opinion, or a global benchmarking exercise, a traditional firm may serve you well. If your need is to change how the business runs and to see results, operator-led work usually fits better. For a longer checklist, see how to choose a business transformation consultant.

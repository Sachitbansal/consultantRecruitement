Prior-career outcomes of GOP practice leads

Each outcome below is shown in the same format: the business context, what was done, what changed, and who did it. Client names are withheld.

Heat exchangers: capacity doubled

  • Business context: a heat exchanger manufacturer needed more output from its existing plant.
  • Intervention: takt, flow and pull implementation, so work is pulled through the plant instead of pushed in batches.
  • Outcome: installed capacity doubled, with no additional floor space.
  • Attribution: prior-career work by the senior practitioners who now lead GOP's manufacturing and supply chain practice, done before GOP. Not a GOP client result. The client is not named.

Press shop: changeover from 70 to 40 minutes

  • Business context: a press shop was losing production hours to long changeovers.
  • Intervention: changeover reduction, separating the work that needs the press stopped from the work that does not, then standardising the routine.
  • Outcome: changeover time cut from 70 to 40 minutes, a 45% reduction, raising available production hours.
  • Attribution: prior-career work by the senior practitioners who now lead GOP's manufacturing and supply chain practice, done before GOP. Not a GOP client result. The client is not named.

Fabrication: throughput up 50%, capacity doubled

  • Business context: a fabrication line could not keep up with demand.
  • Intervention: line and flow improvement on the fabrication line.
  • Outcome: throughput up 50% and productivity up 60%; capacity doubled from 50 to 100 units a month; manpower down 25%; footprint 40% smaller.
  • Attribution: prior-career work by the senior practitioners who now lead GOP's manufacturing and supply chain practice, done before GOP. Not a GOP client result. The client is not named.

Fan manufacturer, new plant of 6 lakh units a month

  • Business context: a fan manufacturer was building a new plant.
  • Intervention: an optimised plant design, compared with a conventional design for the same volume.
  • Outcome: manpower optimised by 32% (from 817 to 558), and the plant built in 40% less space than conventional design.
  • Attribution: prior-career work by the senior practitioners who now lead GOP's manufacturing and supply chain practice, done before GOP. Not a GOP client result. The client is not named.

Materials handling redesign: 60% manpower optimisation

  • Business context: a plant was being brought live.
  • Intervention: conveyorised flow in place of manual movement between operations.
  • Outcome: the plant went live with 60% manpower optimisation.
  • Attribution: prior-career work by the senior practitioners who now lead GOP's manufacturing and supply chain practice, done before GOP. Not a GOP client result. The client is not named.

How to read anyone's results, including ours

Before you rely on any consultant's results, ask a few questions. Was the figure measured the same way before and after, on the same definition? Over what period was it measured, and has it held since? What was the starting condition, and how similar is it to your business? Who did the work: the people who would work on your business, or someone else? In GOP's own engagements, baselines are set during the diagnostic and agreed with the client's team, and progress is tracked weekly on a shared scoreboard.

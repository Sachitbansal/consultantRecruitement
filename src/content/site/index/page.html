What is GOP? GOP (Growth Operating Partners) is the growth consulting and strategy execution vertical of SBSSdigital. Several cohort mates from the IIM Calcutta Executive Programme have come together to build and scale GOP, an operator-led collective of senior professionals with 100+ years of combined hands-on experience. We work alongside promoters of INR 150-500 crore manufacturers to build institution-grade enterprises.

Who GOP helps

GOP helps promoter-led and entrepreneur-led manufacturers with revenue of INR 150-500 crore that want to grow faster, strengthen their commercial engine, and become ready for institutional investment. If your business has strong products and relationships but depends heavily on the promoter, has stalled at a revenue band, or is preparing for a PE, VC or SME listing journey, this is the work we do. We make transformation happen

We do not advise businesses on transformation. We work with them to transform the business. GOP's people are operators who have delivered real results, understand the nuts and bolts on the ground, and know how to work with the people inside a business as enablers of change. We work as operators with the promoter, against KPIs agreed in advance and reviewed every week.

Frequently asked questions

What is GOP?

GOP (Growth Operating Partners) is the growth consulting and strategy execution vertical of SBSSdigital. It is an operator-led collective of senior professionals, several of them from the IIM Calcutta Executive Programme, who work inside promoter-led manufacturing businesses to deliver results-focused transformation.

Who does GOP work with?

GOP works with promoter-led and entrepreneur-led manufacturers, typically with revenue of INR 150-500 crore, across sectors such as fans and motors, electricals, heat exchangers, engineering components, home appliances, paper and packaging, FMCG and consumer durables.

How is GOP different from a consulting firm?

Consulting firms typically deliver plans and decks through analysts. GOP places experienced operators inside your business, working alongside your managers and accountable for KPIs agreed before the work starts.

What does an engagement look like?

It starts with a fixed-scope diagnostic of one to two weeks, followed by a sequenced roadmap with owners, and then implementation inside your team with a weekly review and a KPI scoreboard.

Does GOP work with companies after they take investment?

Yes. GOP works before and after investment, helping companies become investor-ready and then deliver against investor KPIs. Investment se pehle bhi. Investment ke baad bhi.

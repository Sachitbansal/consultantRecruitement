Investment se Pehle Bhi. Investment ke Baad Bhi.

The work of building business value starts before capital is raised and continues after it arrives. GOP does not invest its own money. It prepares the business, helps it raise capital, and helps it deliver afterwards.

Before investment

Build a stronger business foundation:

  • Define growth priorities.
  • Improve profitability and commercial performance.
  • Strengthen management systems and governance.
  • Prepare a credible growth and capital plan.
  • Improve investment readiness.

When capital is required

Prepare for and run the raise:

  • Clarify the business case for capital and its intended deployment.
  • Build the pitch deck and the information investors will ask for.
  • Take the pitch to suitable mid-market PE and VC investors.
  • Support the discussions through diligence, within the agreed mandate and alongside the company's legal and financial advisers.

After investment

Translate capital into business progress:

  • Align leadership around the investment thesis.
  • Establish a prioritised value-creation plan.
  • Improve execution and performance visibility.
  • Monitor agreed business measures.
  • Adapt initiatives as results and circumstances evolve.

Frequently asked questions

What is GOP?

GOP (Growth Operating Partners) is an operator-led growth and value-creation partner and the growth consulting and strategy execution vertical of SBSSdigital. It helps promoter-led businesses scale profitably, become investment-ready, access growth capital and deliver after investment.

Who does GOP work with?

Business promoters and leadership teams, primarily of promoter-led businesses with approximately INR 150-500 crore revenue, as well as prospective investors and PE/VC funds. Most of the team's experience is in manufacturing.

Does GOP invest its own money?

No. GOP does not invest its own capital. It helps businesses prepare a pitch deck, take the pitch to mid-market PE and VC investors and work through the raise. No investment outcome can be guaranteed.

How is GOP different from a consulting firm?

GOP places experienced operators alongside your managers, working on agreed priorities with measures reviewed every week, rather than delivering a report and leaving.

How does an engagement begin?

With a conversation about the business, its objectives and the challenge. For promoters this is usually a free diagnostic conversation. If there is a fit, both sides agree the scope, responsibilities and expected outputs.

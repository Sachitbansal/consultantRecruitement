Who GOP helps

Promoter-led manufacturers with revenue of INR 150-500 crore.

By GOP Editorial · Published 23 October 2026 · Updated 23 October 2026

Who does GOP help? Promoter-led and entrepreneur-led manufacturers with revenue of INR 150-500 crore that want to grow faster, build a stronger commercial engine and become ready for institutional investment. Typical clients have strong products and customer relationships, a promoter who still makes most decisions, and systems built for a smaller company.

Typical situations

  • Revenue has stalled at a particular band and the promoter is the bottleneck.
  • Margins are slipping while volumes grow, and nobody has a clear view of profit by customer or product.
  • The business wants to enter new segments, OEM accounts or export markets without a structured pipeline.
  • The company is preparing for private equity, venture capital, strategic investment or an SME listing.
  • Investment has been taken and the business must deliver against investor KPIs.
  • A supplier to a large OEM needs to raise quality, delivery and management capability.

Sectors with deep exposure

GOP's practice leads have deep exposure to fans and motors, home appliances, electricals and switchgear, heat exchangers, elevators, gears and engineering components, FMCG and household products, consumer durables and electronics, paper and packaging, and e-commerce fulfilment. Across these sectors they have worked with 70+ manufacturers.

What these companies have in common

They have strong products and long relationships, built by a promoter who knows the business intimately. The next stage of growth needs systems, delegation and senior capability beyond what one person can supply. That is the gap GOP fills.

When GOP may not be the right fit

  • You want a strategy report only, with no implementation support.
  • The promoter is not willing to delegate decisions or back changes.
  • The business is at a very early stage without established operations.

Telling you this honestly at the start saves everyone's time. If your situation fits, make an enquiry.
